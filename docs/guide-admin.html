Admin / Foreman Guide

You run the day-to-day. You can see and do almost everything — except deleting money records and managing accounts.

Signing in

Use the username and password the Owner gave you, on the main login page. (That's the office side. The crew phone-and-PIN portal is only for field workers.)

What you can do

  • See everything — jobs, money, and compliance.
  • Create & edit jobs, proposals, contracts, invoices, and change orders; send them to customers.
  • Log expenses, income, and payments.
  • Approve crew time on the Time Clock (turns a clocked day into pay + job cost).
  • Assign tasks to workers, and set a worker's phone & PIN.
  • Manage permits, licenses, subs, waivers, vehicles, and reminders.
  • Enable and share the client portal.

What you can't do

  • Delete or void money records (payments, expenses, income, invoices, contracts, proposals, change orders).
  • Delete a job, worker, or customer.
  • Manage user accounts.

Need one of these? Ask the Owner. To fix a mistake, edit the record instead of deleting it.

If you try an owner-only action, the app stops you with "That action is owner-only." Nothing is changed.

Common tasks

1
Approve a worker's day: Money → Time Clock → check the in/out times and the map pin (where they punched) → Approve. That pays them one day at their daily rate.
2
Assign a task: Jobs → open the job → Job Hub → Crew tasks → type the task, pick the worker, Assign. It shows up on their phone.
3
Record an expense: Money → Expenses → pick the job, category, amount, date, snap a receipt photo, Add Expense.
4
Record a worker payment (check): Money → Payments → Worker, Amount, Date, Method = Check, Check #, photo, Add Payment.
Workers are paid by the day. The time clock is attendance — who showed up, when, and where — not hourly math.
