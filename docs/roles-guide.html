Roles & Access Guide

Who can do what in the system, and how to set each person up. Three roles: Owner, Admin / Foreman, and Worker (Crew).

There are two separate ways to get into the system:

Office login  A username & password at the main sign-in page. This is for the Owner and Admins/Foremen — the people who run jobs, money, and paperwork.

Crew login  A phone number & PIN on the crew portal. This is for Workers in the field. No username, no password — like a digital time clock. Workers never see the office side.

The three roles

Owner

Office login
You. Full control of the entire system — everything an Admin can do, plus the sensitive actions below.
Owner-only powers
  • Manage user accounts — create Admin/Foreman logins, change roles, activate or deactivate people (Tools → Users)
  • Delete & void records — delete a job, worker, customer, proposal, contract, invoice, change order, payment, expense, or income
Plus everything an Admin can do

Create and edit jobs, proposals, contracts, invoices, permits, licenses, subs, waivers; log work and expenses; approve crew time; assign tasks; run the client portal.

Admin / Foreman

Office login
A trusted office/lead user the Owner creates. Runs the day-to-day, but can't delete money records or manage accounts.
Can do
  • See everything — full visibility of jobs, money, and compliance
  • Create & edit jobs, proposals, contracts, invoices, change orders
  • Log work, expenses, income, and payments
  • Approve crew time on the Time Clock (turns hours into labor cost & payroll)
  • Assign tasks to workers; set a worker's phone & PIN
  • Manage permits, licenses, subs, waivers, vehicles, reminders; run the client portal
Cannot do
  • Delete or void financial records (payments, expenses, income, invoices, contracts, proposals, change orders)
  • Delete a job, worker, or customer
  • Manage user accounts (create logins, change roles)
If an Admin tries an owner-only action, the system stops them with "That action is owner-only." and sends them home. Nothing is deleted.

Worker (Crew)

Crew login
Your field crew. They use the crew portal on their phone with a phone number + PIN. They only ever see their own work.
Can do
  • Sign in with their phone number and PIN
  • See the tasks assigned to them individually
  • Start a task, mark it done, or reopen it
  • Clock in to one assigned job at a time, and clock out
Cannot do
  • See other workers' tasks, or any jobs/money/office pages
  • Assign tasks or approve their own time
  • Log in on the office side (no username/password)

How to set people up

Add an Admin / Foreman (Owner only)

1
Go to Tools → Users.
2
Enter a username and a temporary password, then create the account. New office accounts are Admin/Foreman by default.
3
Share the username/password with them. They sign in at the main login page. Ask them to change the password.
Keep Owner to yourself (and maybe one trusted backup). Only the Owner can delete money records or manage accounts.

Set up a Worker for crew login

1
Go to Money → Workers and add the worker (or edit an existing one).
2
Fill in their Phone number and a 4–6 digit PIN. That phone + PIN is how they sign in — there's no username or password.
3
Give them the crew portal link and their PIN. They open it on their phone and sign in with their phone number + PIN.
Forgot PIN? Any Owner/Admin just re-sets the PIN on the worker's record. The old PIN stops working.

Assign work to a crew member

1
Open the job: Jobs → (pick the job) → Job Hub.
2
In the Crew tasks panel, type the task, choose the worker, set a due date (optional), and click Assign.
3
The task appears in that worker's crew portal. They Start it and Mark it done from their phone; you see the status update on the Job Hub.

The daily crew flow

1
Worker opens the crew portal, signs in with phone + PIN.
2
Worker picks the job and taps Clock in (one job at a time). They work through their tasks — Start / Mark done.
3
Worker taps Clock out at the end.
4
Owner / Admin opens Money → Time Clock, reviews the hours, and clicks Approve. Approving turns those hours into a labor cost on that job — and it shows up in Money → Payroll for the week.
Labor flows in automatically from approved crew time. The manual "record a labor payment" checkbox on Work Logs is only for traditional workers who don't clock in — so nothing gets counted twice.
