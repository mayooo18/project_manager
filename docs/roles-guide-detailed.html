1 · Two ways to sign in

The system has two completely separate front doors. A person uses one or the other — never both.

Office login  A username & password at the main sign-in page. For the Owner and Admins/Foremen — the people who manage jobs, money, proposals, invoices, and paperwork.

Crew login  A phone number & PIN on the crew portal. For Workers in the field. No username, no password — it works like a digital time clock. Workers only ever see their own tasks and the clock; they never reach the office pages.

Why two doors? It keeps sensitive money and customer data away from phones in the field, and makes clocking in dead-simple for the crew — just a phone number and a PIN.

2 · Owner

Owner

Office login

You (and optionally one trusted backup). Complete control of the whole system.

Owner-only powers (nobody else can do these)
  • Manage user accounts — create Admin/Foreman logins, change someone's role, and activate or deactivate accounts (Tools → Users).
  • Delete & void records — remove a job, worker, or customer, and delete any money record: proposal, contract, invoice, change order, payment, expense, or income.
Everything an Admin can do, too

Create/edit jobs, proposals, contracts, invoices, and change orders; log work, expenses, income, and payments; approve crew time; assign tasks; manage permits, licenses, subs, waivers, vehicles, and reminders; run the client portal.

Keep the Owner login to yourself. Because only the Owner can delete money records, an accidental or malicious deletion by staff is impossible — they simply don't have the button.

3 · Admin / Foreman

Admin / Foreman

Office login

A trusted office user or lead the Owner creates. Runs the day-to-day. New office accounts are Admin by default.

Can do
  • See everything — full visibility of jobs, money, and compliance.
  • Sales & billing — create and edit proposals, contracts, invoices, and change orders; send them to customers.
  • Money in/out — log expenses, income, and payments; record work.
  • Crew & time — assign tasks to workers, set a worker's phone & PIN, and approve crew time on the Time Clock (this turns hours into labor cost and payroll).
  • Compliance — manage permits & inspections, licenses, subcontractors, waivers/certificates, vehicles, and reminders.
  • Client portal — enable, share, and revoke the customer's private link.
Cannot do
  • Delete or void financial records (payments, expenses, income, invoices, contracts, proposals, change orders).
  • Delete a job, worker, or customer.
  • Manage user accounts (create logins, change roles, deactivate people).
If an Admin attempts an owner-only action, the system blocks it with "That action is owner-only." and returns them to the dashboard. Nothing is changed or deleted — the limit is enforced by the software, not just by trust.

4 · Worker (Crew)

Worker (Crew)

Crew login

Your field crew. They use the crew portal on their phone with a phone number and PIN.

Can do
  • Sign in with their phone number + PIN.
  • See the tasks assigned to them individually — not anyone else's.
  • Start a task, mark it done, or reopen it.
  • Clock in to one assigned job at a time, and clock out.
Cannot do
  • See other workers' tasks, or any jobs, money, or office pages.
  • Assign tasks or approve their own time.
  • Sign in on the office side — they have no username or password.
A worker can only clock into a job they've been assigned a task on. Assign at least one task on a job and that job becomes available in their "Clock in" list.

6 · Add an Admin / Foreman (Owner only)

1
Sign in as Owner and go to Tools → Users.
2
Enter a username and a temporary password, then create the account. New office accounts are created as Admin/Foreman.
3
Give the person their username and temporary password. They sign in at the main login page and should change the password right away.
4
To remove access later, deactivate the account on the Users page (don't delete history). Deactivated users can't sign in.

7 · Set up a Worker for crew login

1
Go to Money → Workers and add the worker (or edit an existing one).
2
Enter their Daily Rate, Phone number, and a 4–6 digit PIN. The phone + PIN is their login — there is no username or password.
3
Send them the crew portal link and tell them their PIN. They open it on their phone and sign in with phone number + PIN.
The daily rate is how labor cost is figured: the system uses daily rate ÷ 8 as the hourly rate when it turns approved clock time into a labor cost on the job.

8 · Assign work to a crew member

1
Open the job: Jobs → (pick the job) → Job Hub.
2
In the Crew tasks panel, type the task, choose the worker, set a due date (optional), and click Assign.
3
The task shows up in that worker's crew portal. As they Start it and Mark it done, the status updates on the Job Hub so you can see progress.

9 · The daily crew flow

1
Worker opens the crew portal and signs in with phone + PIN.
2
Worker picks the job and taps Clock in (one job at a time), works the tasks, and taps Clock out at the end of the day.
3
Owner / Admin opens Money → Time Clock, reviews the hours, and clicks Approve. Approving posts that time as a labor cost on the job.
4
Approved labor shows up in Money → Payroll for the week (Mon–Sun), so you can see what everyone is owed.
Labor flows in automatically from approved crew time. The manual "record a labor payment" option is only for traditional workers who don't clock in — that way nothing is counted twice.

10 · FAQ & troubleshooting

A worker forgot their PIN.

Any Owner or Admin opens Money → Workers, edits the worker, and sets a new PIN. The old one stops working immediately.

A worker's job isn't showing when they try to clock in.

They can only clock into jobs they have a task on. Assign them at least one task on that job (Job Hub → Crew tasks), and it will appear.

An Admin needs to delete a wrong payment or expense.

Admins can't delete money records — that's owner-only, on purpose. Ask the Owner to delete it, or the Admin can edit the record to correct it instead.

Someone left the company.

Office staff: deactivate their account (Tools → Users). Crew: set them to Inactive on the Workers page — they can no longer clock in, and their history is preserved.

Can a foreman see profit and pricing?

Yes. Admin/Foreman has full visibility of jobs and money. If you don't want a lead seeing everything, keep them as a Worker (crew) instead — crew only see their own tasks and the clock.

11 · Glossary

Job
A project you're building. Jobs are created automatically when an approved proposal becomes a contract, and everything (money, permits, tasks, files) hangs off the job's "Job Hub."
Proposal
A quote you send a customer to approve and e-sign.
Contract
The signed agreement, with a draw schedule (how the job gets billed in stages).
Change Order
An add-on to a signed contract, sent to the customer to approve and sign.
Invoice
A bill sent to the customer; can be paid online.
Client Portal
A private, no-login web link you share so the customer can see their job status, invoices, and documents.
Time Punch
A crew clock-in/clock-out. Once approved, it becomes a labor cost on the job.
Work Log
A manual record of days worked, for workers who don't clock in on the app.
Payroll
The weekly (Mon–Sun) total of what each worker is owed, built from approved time and work logs.
