Owner Guide

You have full control. This covers the things only you can do, and your regular routine.

What only you can do

Two powers are reserved for the Owner — no Admin/Foreman has them:

  • Manage user accounts — create Admin/Foreman logins, change roles, and activate/deactivate people (Tools → Users).
  • Delete & void records — delete a job, worker, or customer, and delete any money record (payment, expense, income, invoice, contract, proposal, change order).

Everything else, you share with your Admins/Foremen: jobs, proposals, contracts, invoices, expenses, payments, time approval, tasks, compliance, and the client portal.

Keep your Owner login to yourself. Because only you can delete money records, staff can never remove or alter financial history — they simply don't have the button.

Setting up your team

1
Add an Admin/Foreman: Tools → Users → enter a username + temporary password. New office accounts are Admin by default. Have them change the password.
2
Add a worker: Money → Workers → enter their Daily Rate, Phone, and a 4–6 digit PIN. The phone + PIN is how they log into the crew portal on their phone.
3
Assign work: open a job (Jobs → Job Hub → Crew tasks), type a task, pick the worker, and Assign. A worker can only clock into jobs they have a task on.

Your regular routine

1
Approve time. Money → Time Clock — review each crew day (in/out times and the map pin showing where they punched), then Approve. Approving pays that worker one day at their daily rate and adds it to the job's cost.
2
Check payroll. Money → Payroll shows what each worker is owed for the week (days × daily rate).
3
Watch the money. The dashboard and each Job Hub show income, expenses, labor, and profit per job.
Workers are paid by the day. The time clock is for attendance — who showed up, when, and where — not for hourly math.
